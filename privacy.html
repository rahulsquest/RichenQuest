📋 Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide to us, and some information automatically when you use our website.

  • Full name, email address, phone number (from our contact / counselling form)

  • Preferred study destination, field of study, study level
  • Passport details (only when required for visa assistance)
  • Financial information (only when required for scholarship or blocked-account applications)
  • Communications you send us via email, WhatsApp, or our contact form

⚙️ How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information for the following purposes:

  • To provide education counselling and advisory services
  • To assess your eligibility for universities and scholarship programmes
  • To prepare and submit your university applications on your behalf
  • To assist with visa applications and embassy documentation
  • To send you relevant updates about application deadlines, scholarship news, and events
  • To respond to your enquiries submitted via our contact form or WhatsApp
  • To improve our website functionality and content
  • To comply with legal obligations applicable in Pakistan
✅

We will never sell your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes. Your information is used solely to provide you with our consultancy services.

4

🤝 Information Sharing

We may share your information with third parties only in the following circumstances, and only as necessary:

  • Partner Universities: When submitting your application, we share your academic and personal details with the universities you have chosen to apply to.
  • Embassies & Visa Authorities: Visa applications require sharing relevant documents with the respective country's embassy or immigration authority.
  • Scholarship Bodies: If you apply for scholarships (e.g. DAAD, Swedish Institute), your application is shared directly with that body.
  • Service Providers: We may use trusted third-party tools (e.g. email services, website hosting) who process data on our behalf under confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or regulatory authority.
5

🛡️ Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These include:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmitted through our website
  • Restricted access to personal data — only staff who need it to provide your services
  • Secure storage of physical documents in locked facilities
  • Regular review of our data handling practices
⚠️

While we take all reasonable precautions, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

6

Our website uses cookies — small text files stored on your device — to improve your browsing experience. We use the following types:

7

🔗 Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external websites including university portals, scholarship platforms, government immigration websites, and accommodation services. These third-party sites have their own privacy policies and we are not responsible for their content or practices. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any site you visit.

8

👶 Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for students who are at least 16 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16 without verifiable parental consent. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will take steps to remove that information.

9

✊ Your Rights

You have the following rights with respect to your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal obligations
  • Objection: Object to certain processing of your data
  • Withdrawal of Consent: Withdraw consent for communications at any time
  • Data Portability: Request transfer of your data in a machine-readable format

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@richenquest.com. We will respond within 30 days.

10

🗓️ Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations:

  • Active client files: Retained for the duration of the service plus 3 years
  • Enquiry / contact form data: Retained for 12 months if no service commenced
  • Application documents: Retained for 5 years to assist with reapplication needs
  • Financial records: Retained for 7 years as required by Pakistani tax law

After the retention period, data is securely deleted or anonymised.

11

🌍 International Data Transfers

When we submit your application to Global universities or scholarship bodies, your personal data will be transferred internationally. These transfers are necessary to provide our services and are made with your explicit consent when you engage us to submit applications on your behalf.

Global countries are subject to GDPR and offer a high standard of data protection. We ensure all transfers are made to institutions with appropriate data safeguards in place.

  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page
  • Sending an email notification to active clients
  • Displaying a notice on our website homepage

We encourage you to review this page periodically. Your continued use of our services after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
